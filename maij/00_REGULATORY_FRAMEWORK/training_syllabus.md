# MAIJ Training Syllabus (Draft Skeleton)

**Document:** TRN-MAIJ-001 · **Version:** 1.0 (Draft) · **Target standard:** Part 135 training program principles

This syllabus defines the training required before any person performs an operational role. A unique MAIJ design feature: the **game-derived simulation stack** (route AI vectors, IFR-style schedule management) forms the qualification simulator for dispatchers and remote pilots.

## 1. Remote-Pilot Curricula

* **Initial:** aircraft systems, GOM (MAN-MAIJ-OPS-002), Part 135 rules, aerodynamics and performance for the 1,600–1,750 lb class, DAA and encrypted-C2 procedures, Remote ID, SMS fundamentals, and practical simulator qualification.
* **Recurrent (annual):** emergencies (lost link, degraded energy, thermal events), regulatory updates, and simulator evaluations.
* **Differences/transition training** when airframe, propulsion, or battery variants change (the volatile-elements register).

## 2. Dispatcher and Operations-Control Curricula

* Route network geography (ICE/RE/S-Bahn tiers), simulated AI-vector scheduling, public-transit-as-IFR-clearance procedures, weather decision-making, and cold-chain dispatch checks.

## 3. Maintenance-Personnel Training

* CAMP execution (CAMP-MAIJ-001), battery/megawatt-charging safety, propeller and DAA-sensor maintenance, and documentation standards.

## 4. Human Factors and CRM

* Fatigue management for the one-person-supervising-many-aircraft operations model; alarm and telemetry ergonomics; SMS reporting culture.

## 5. Records

* Qualification files retained for the duration of employment plus three years.
