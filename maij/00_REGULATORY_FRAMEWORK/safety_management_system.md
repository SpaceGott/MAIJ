# MAIJ Safety Management System Manual (Draft Skeleton)

**Document:** SMS-MAIJ-001 · **Version:** 1.0 (Draft) · **Target standard:** 14 CFR Part 5 principles

## 1. Safety Policy and Objectives

Safety is the first consideration in every MAIJ operation. The Accountable Executive is the Director of Operations. Reporting of hazards is protected and expected; the safety manager operates independently.

## 2. Safety Risk Management (SRM)

* Formal hazard identification and risk assessment for every route, aircraft variant, and procedural change — with special controls for BVLOS (DAA and encrypted-C2 failure modes), battery thermal risk, and food-safety failure modes (cross-referenced to SOP-MAIJ-CC-001).
* Safety risk analysis and assessment matrices; documentation retained indefinitely for FAA review.

## 3. Safety Assurance

* Employee reporting system; mandatory investigation of incidents (lost link, degraded energy, forced landing, cold-chain breach).
* Internal audit calendar; external audit coordination during Part 135 certification; continuing airworthiness data (from CAMP-MAIJ-001) fed into trend analysis.

## 4. Safety Promotion

* SMS orientation at onboarding (via TRN-MAIJ-001); safety newsletters; lessons-learned library.

## 5. Emergency Response Plan (ERP)

* Incident command structure; public-safety and FAA notification trees; family-care procedures; drills semi-annually.
