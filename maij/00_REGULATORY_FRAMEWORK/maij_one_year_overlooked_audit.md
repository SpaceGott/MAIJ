# MAIJ One-Year Overlooked-Items Audit (November 2026 – November 2027)

An honest gap analysis of everything the current plan touches versus what it leaves **untouched or overlooked** over the next twelve months across the MAIJ venture, the Roblox games, the data strategy, and the founder's life calendar.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The current plan (migration + 11-category data taxonomy + 4-phase micro-roadmap) executes the **digital and simulation layer** very well: files staged, applications audited, games sequenced, formulas formalized. What is largely untouched for the next twelve months is the **physical, regulatory, legal, and operational layer** of the real-world mission — plus the fixed calendar event of the year: **the CBYX/PPP fellowship ending in June 2027**.

The ten gaps below are prioritized by how much they could invalidate or delay the short-term mission if ignored.

---

## 2. The Ten Untouched or Overlooked Areas

### 2.1 The Physical Hardware Reality (Severity: HIGH)

The entire plan lives in simulation. There is currently zero budget, timeline, or procurement plan for the real **concept aircraft** (the ~20 lb payload concept drone from your earlier economics), camera sensors, flight controller hardware, or battery systems for the real-world Part 107 demonstration flights. Simulations produce training data, but the FAA and customers need to see real metal land with real lettuce and beef. If you reach June 2027 with four great games and no physical drone on the build calendar, the short-term mission is materially delayed.

*Next action (Q1 2027):* write a physical-hardware procurement and bench-test plan with a named airframe (buy or build), a sensor list, and a Part 107 flight test schedule on private NC property.

### 2.2 FAA and Regulatory Filing Clocks (Severity: HIGH)

Three regulatory calendars exist whether the plan tracks them or not: **FAA Part 108** (routine BVLOS rulemaking) has been at the White House review office since July 2026 and a final rule could drop within twelve months; **Part 107** currency and any waivers; and **Part 135 air carrier certification**, which itself takes 12–24 months — the clock has not even started. None of the applications, waiver strategies, or FAA pre-application paperwork are on any calendar.

*Next action (Q2 2027):* create a regulatory calendar; consult an aviation attorney on the Part 107 → Part 107.41 airspace authorization → Part 135 pathway and the cost estimate for certification.

### 2.3 The Cold-Chain and Meat-Inspection Compliance Path (Severity: HIGH)

MAIJ's mission explicitly includes **inspected meat**, which gives the **NC Department of Agriculture's meat & poultry inspection division** regulatory jurisdiction, alongside county health departments for restaurant receiving. The plan currently has no written standard operating procedures for the cold chain, no temperature-logger certification plan, no Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP)-style plan, and no meeting scheduled with NC food-safety regulators.

*Next action (Q1 2027):* draft the one-page cold-chain SOP; request an informational meeting with the NC Department of Agriculture meat-inspection division.

### 2.4 Trademark and Parody Risk for "Costlo" (Severity: MEDIUM)

A public, potentially monetized Roblox game called "Costlo" parodying Costco Wholesale carries real trademark and dilution exposure if it gains traction — the exact opposite of what you want while the venture is fragile. There is no clearance review, no parody-safe name variant ("FreshPort", "BarnBox"), and no counsel consultation budgeted.

*Next action (Q4 2026):* run a trademark clearance check; register parody-safe alternatives; file MAIJ wordmarks for the real venture.

### 2.5 The CBYX Transition — the Fixed Life Calendar Event (Severity: HIGH)

Your CBYX/PPP fellowship ends **June 2027** (~8 months away). Untouched: visa transition, graduation timing at TU Dortmund versus ECSU, relocation logistics back to the United States, and whether MAIJ can operate legally while you are based in Germany (a .us domain with a Microsoft 365 tenant exists, but the legal entity, bank account, and NC corporate registration are not formalized).

*Next action (Q1 2027):* form the NC legal entity (LLC or corporation), open the business bank account, and define the post-June 2027 operating base (Raleigh/Durham corridor aligns best with the hub map).

### 2.6 Player Data Governance and Child Safety (Severity: MEDIUM)

The language-learning game is aimed at learners on Roblox — a platform with a very high share of minors. Untouched: a COPPA/GDPR-K compliance check, a written data-retention policy for the anonymized AI-usage-ratio logs, parental-notice terms for any classroom use, and confirmation that the game's telemetry meets your own school-to-sky firewall rule (no school-system join of game data).

*Next action (Q4 2026):* draft the two-page player-data governance policy before any public launch of the language game.

### 2.7 Business, Capital, and Insurance Foundations (Severity: HIGH)

MAIJ currently has no incorporated entity, no business bank account, no EIN, no cap-table plan, and no aviation or cargo insurance coverage. You cannot invoice a restaurant, receive pilot grant funds, or fly a commercial demo without these. The game phases can generate revenue only if there is a legal entity to receive it.

*Next action (Q1 2027):* form the entity, obtain an EIN, open the bank account, and request insurance quotes for Part 107 commercial cargo operations.

### 2.8 Sim-to-Real Transfer Validation (Severity: MEDIUM)

The Phase 3 rate formulas will be calibrated on Roblox player data, which has never been validated against any real delivery operation. There is no budgeted validation step — for example, comparing the model against one week of public Flytrex/Zipline operating data or a tiny ground-van pilot on real NC roads. Without this, the rate models are theory calibrated on a game.

*Next action (Q2 2027):* collect real ground-delivery pricing benchmarks from 10+ NC restaurants and run a five-day ground-van pilot to validate the cost model with real receipts.

### 2.9 Team Sustainability and Solo-Founder Scope (Severity: MEDIUM)

Four games, five interlocking Costlo systems, formula modules, data pipelines, a thesis, exams, and a fellowship are a high solo workload. Untouched: co-founder or contributor recruiting, scoped milestones that assume solo capacity, and a per-module definition-of-done that forces shipping rather than polishing.

*Next action (Q4 2026):* cap Phase 1 at eight weeks with the definition-of-done from the roadmap; explore one student co-builder at TU Dortmund or ECSU.

### 2.10 The Education-Venture Track Outreach (Severity: MEDIUM)

Your school-to-sky skill defines a school agreement stack (discovery note → Design-Partner MOA → authorized pilot contract) for taking the language game into classrooms. The plan describes the game but contains no outreach calendar, no target school districts (in NC or Germany), and no meeting schedule for the next twelve months.

*Next action (Q1 2027):* identify three target districts (e.g., in the Triangle corridor) and send the first discovery notes after Phase 1's MVP is playable.

If both tracks are not running in parallel, the simulation track will succeed while the mission waits. The 11-category taxonomy and the PowerShell migration script are already ready to receive both tracks — folders 08, 09, 10, and 11 will simply fill faster and more honestly once the physical track is producing data too.
