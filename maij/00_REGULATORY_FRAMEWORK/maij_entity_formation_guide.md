# MAIJ — North Carolina Entity Formation & Business Foundations Guide

A practical, step-by-step guide to formalizing **MAIJ** as a North Carolina business entity before any real-world commercial delivery or grant funding.

---

## 1. Entity Choice: NC LLC vs. Corporation

| Factor | NC LLC (Recommended) | NC C-Corporation |
|---|---|---|
| **Forming cost** | $125 filing fee (online) | $125 + higher ongoing costs |
| **Annual report** | $200/year (online) | $25-ish plus franchise tax reports |
| **Best for** | Solo/small-team ventures; farm-and-restaurant customer contracts | Ventures raising institutional venture capital immediately |
| **Simplicity** | Minimal formalities, single-member tax pass-through | Board, bylaws, stock ledger required |

**Recommendation:** Form a **single-member NC LLC** now ("MAIJ LLC" or "MAIJ Logistics LLC" — check name availability at the NC Secretary of State business registry). Convert to a C-Corp later only if a Delaware structure is demanded by institutional investors.

## 2. Formation Checklist (One-Week Path)

| Step | Action | Cost | Link/Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name check: search the NC Secretary of State Business Registry for "MAIJ" name variants | Free | [sosnc.gov] business search |
| 2 | Choose a **registered agent** (a physical NC address; use a service if your base is still in Germany) | ~$100–150/year | Northwest Registered Agent, LegalZoom, or a trusted NC resident |
| 3 | File **Articles of Organization** online at the NC SOS portal | $125 | [sosnc.gov] portal |
| 4 | Get an **EIN** (Employer Identification Number) from the IRS online (takes 10 minutes) | Free | [irs.gov] EIN assistant |
| 5 | Adopt an **Operating Agreement** (single-member, one page) — required by banks | Free template | Keep signed PDF in drive F: `07_Documentation_and_Text_Data` |
| 6 | Open a **business checking account** (Mercury, Relay, or a NC-based bank like First Citizens) | Free–low fees | Mercury.com or local NC bank |
| 7 | Register for NC state tax accounts (if hiring) at the **NC Department of Revenue** | Free | [ncdor.gov] |
| 8 | Obtain the **county/municipal privilege-tax or zoning permit** if storing aircraft at a physical site | Varies | County planning office |

## 3. Insurance Request (Send Q1 2027)

Cargo and aviation insurance is required before any commercial flight. Use this request email template:

> **To:** an aviation-insurance broker (e.g., Global Aerospace, AIG Aerospace, or a broker specializing in commercial UAS)
> **Subject:** Commercial UAS cargo operation — insurance quotation request (NC)
> **Body:** MAIJ LLC is a North Carolina-based autonomous drone freight venture transporting perishable food from small farms to restaurants under FAA Part 107 (later Part 135/108). We request quotes for: (1) commercial general liability; (2) aircraft hull liability (MTOW under 55 lb); (3) cargo/transit spoilage insurance; (4) non-owned aircraft coverage for pilot contractors. Operations: NC Triangle/Piedmont, daylight VLOS, under 400 ft, insulated passive-cooled payload bays.

* File an **NC trademark** for the MAIJ wordmark at the NC SOS ($75 per class) for immediate in-state protection; file a **US federal trademark (USPTO)** when budget allows (Classes 39: delivery services; 42: technology services).
